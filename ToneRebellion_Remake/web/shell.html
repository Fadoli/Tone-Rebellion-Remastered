Tone Rebellion Remastered

Play the 1997 strategy game of floating islands, tones and the Leviathan in your browser, with your own copy of the game.

Start playing

Point this page at your copy of Tone Rebellion. Any of these works:

  • The game CD, or a copy of it: the folder that holds THING1 and THING2.
  • An installed game: the folder with TONE00.COB and TONE01.COB (usually C:\Program Files\Tone).
  • A CD image: a .iso or .img file. You can also drag and drop it onto this page.

Your files never leave your computer. They are copied into this browser's own storage, so next time a Play button takes you straight in.

What is this?

Tone Rebellion (The Logic Factory, 1997) is a real-time strategy game. You lead a tribe of Floaters, build on islands drifting in the sky, gather tone and fight the creatures of the Leviathan to win back your world.

This is a fan-made remaster: a new engine, written from scratch, that reads the original game's data and reproduces its rules, screens and sounds. It runs on modern Windows and in a recent desktop browser, with no emulator or old system needed.

It is a hobby project. The game is playable from the title screen to the end of a game, but some behaviours are still missing or approximated; the open tasks list what remains. New to the game? The game guide explains what unlocks when, the glyphs, the difficulty levels and every unit's stats.

Why do I need the original game?

The artwork, sounds and music belong to their owners, so they are not included here. The remaster brings only the engine; your copy of the game brings everything you see and hear.

What the remaster adds

  • Any window size, with upscaling filters (xBR, HQx, ScaleFX and more) and a smoother display up to 120 Hz.
  • Optional widescreen island view.
  • Comfort: smooth and edge scrolling, alerts you can jump to, rebindable shortcuts, quicksave, quickload and autosave.
  • Your old saves from the original game still load.
  • New menus in five languages: English, French, German, Italian and Spanish.

Each change can be turned off to get the original behaviour back. The full list is in What's new.

Saves and privacy

Saves and settings live in this browser. Clearing this site's browsing data removes them, along with the copied game files. Use Export saves to keep a .zip backup, and Import saves to restore it or move it to another computer.

Nothing is uploaded: no account, no tracking. Everything runs on your machine.

Prefer a desktop version?

The Windows version finds the game by itself (installed folder, CD, or image next to it) and adds network play and the intro movie.
